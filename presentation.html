2. THE PROBLEM

Why Traveling to the Hospital is Exhausting & Painful

Four critical barriers faced by bedridden, elderly, and post-surgery patients daily:

01

Severe Mobility Barrier

Transporting frail or paralyzed patients through dense traffic, ambulances, and stairs causes severe physical trauma and risk of injury.

02

Minor Clinical Friction

Patients must endure 3-hour hospital OPD waits just for simple 10-minute procedures like IV drips, wound dressings, injections, or catheter care.

03

Family Caregiver Burnout

Working family members struggle to manage daily sponge baths, diaper changes, and feeding for aging parents with professional dignity.

04

Rehabilitation Dropout

Over 60% of stroke and orthopedic patients stop crucial physiotherapy sessions because traveling to clinics daily is impossible.
