Why Traveling to the Hospital is Exhausting & Painful

Four critical barriers faced by bedridden, elderly, and post-surgery patients daily:

01

Severe Mobility Barrier

Transporting frail or paralyzed patients through dense traffic, ambulances, and stairs causes severe physical trauma and risk of injury.

02

Patients must endure 3-hour hospital OPD waits just for simple 10-minute procedures like IV saline infusion, wound dressings, or catheter changes.

03

Family Caregiver Burnout

Over 80% of urban working family members struggle to manage daily sponge baths, diaper changes, and feeding for aging parents with dignity.

04

Rehabilitation Dropout

Over 60% of stroke and orthopedic post-fracture patients stop crucial physiotherapy sessions because traveling to clinics daily is impossible.
